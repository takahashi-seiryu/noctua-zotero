Note guide

Notes are made of blocks. Write in LaTeX-flavoured text, sketch by hand, drop in images, and cite papers from your Zotero library. Everything saves automatically.

Write

  • Click a text block to edit its source. The rendered result appears beside it (or below, depending on Settings → Preview while editing).
  • With the preview beside the source, the block you are editing widens to up to 90% of the window, even if Note width is smaller.
  • Tab indents the line (or every selected line) and ⇧+Tab outdents it. Press Esc first if you want Tab to move focus away.
  • Press Esc or ⌘/Ctrl+Enter to finish editing.
  • Brackets, quotes and dollar signs pair automatically. ⌘/Ctrl+B and I toggle bold and italic.

Math and structure

  • $inline$ and $$display$$ math, plus \section{…} headings.
  • equation, align, gather, cases and matrices work, with automatic numbering; \label{key} and \ref{key} / \eqref{key} cross-reference them.
  • Markdown basics also work at the start of a line: # Heading, - item, 1. item, > quote, ---, and inline **bold**, *italic*, `code`.
  • Shared \newcommand macros go in the LaTeX preamble (⋯ menu).
  • Tables: \begin{tabular}{lcr} … \end{tabular}, a table environment with \caption, or | pipe | tables |.

Add blocks

  • Hover between blocks (or below the last one) and choose Text, Drawing or Image.
  • ⌘/Ctrl+⇧+D adds a drawing after the current block.
  • Paste or drop PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF or SVG images to add them.
  • Paste a YouTube link on its own line to show a thumbnail card; click it to play the video inside the note (press play once more in the player). The “Watch on YouTube” link opens it in your browser. The thumbnail loads from i.ytimg.com, and the player from youtube.com once you click.
  • A text block after a drawing can sit beside it; use the block menu to switch.

Arrange blocks

  • Drag the handle (⋮⋮) to reorder, or press Alt+↑ / ↓ while the handle is focused.
  • Click or right-click the handle for move, duplicate and delete.

Cite

  • Type \cite{ and a list of your Zotero library appears under the caret. Keep typing to filter by title, author, year or citation key; ↑ ↓ to move, Enter or Tab to insert, Esc to close.
  • Citations link back to the item, and a reference list is built at the end of the note.

Assistant

  • Open it with the sparkles button or ⇧⌘/CtrlK. It runs the Claude or Codex command-line tool already installed on your computer.
  • Choose the model and reasoning level with the selector next to the send button. The lists come from the provider itself, in the order it recommends: Claude Code's own model list for your account and the Codex CLI's catalog. The main models come first; older versions are under Show more. Default leaves the choice to the CLI. With Codex, the lightning button turns Fast mode on or off (faster responses, more usage) for models that offer it.
  • Type / for commands (/new, /history, /model, /provider) and the skills installed for the selected CLI, or $ for skills only. A skill you pick is sent along with your message; the assistant can only use its file tools, so skills that need more cannot run.
  • For a note under a paper with a PDF, the assistant can also read the paper (full text and your highlights) as reference while it edits the note, so you can ask it to use a section or check a claim. Turn this off in Settings → Noctua Notes → Provider.
  • You can attach files and images. The assistant proposes edits; nothing changes until you press Apply, and one Undo reverts it.
  • Past conversations for this note are under the history button.

Save, undo and share

  • The status at the top shows Saving and Saved. ⌘/Ctrl+S saves immediately.
  • ⌘/Ctrl+Z undoes and ⌘/Ctrl+⇧+Z redoes, across the whole note.
  • Import and export are in Zotero’s Tools menu and in each item’s right-click menu.
